Our Mission

The ministry of Huron Helping Hands Food Pantry is to provide food to help the hungry in our community. We serve low-income families, single parents, those who are unemployed, the elderly, the physically disabled, and anyone facing temporary hard times. Everyone deserves access to nutritious food, and we're here to help — with dignity, respect, and open arms.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve residents of Huron, Berlin Heights, and the 44839 zip code area. If you're unsure whether you qualify, please reach out — we're here to help, not to judge.
