How Simply Give Works

1

Shop at Meijer

Visit your local participating Meijer store during the campaign.

2

Buy a $10 Card

Ask the cashier to add a $10 Simply Give donation card to your order.

3

Meijer Matches

Meijer converts your donation into a food-only gift card and matches it.

4

We Get Groceries

We receive the gift cards and use them to stock our shelves with nutritious food!

Other Ways to Participate

🏢 Business & Group Campaigns

Rally your coworkers, church group, or organization to participate in Simply Give! Coordinate a group shopping trip on Double Match Day, or collect donations and have one person purchase cards on behalf of the group. Contact us for more information on organizing a group effort.

📣 Spread the Word

Share this campaign with friends, family, and on social media. The more people who know about Simply Give, the more food we can provide to families in need. Every card purchased makes a difference!

🗓️ Mark Your Calendar

Set reminders for the Double Match Days (March 7 & 21, 2026). These are the best days to maximize your contribution — your $10 becomes $30 for our pantry!

Year-Round Initiatives

🍂 Holiday Food Drives

Throughout the year, we coordinate special food drives for Thanksgiving, Christmas, and other holidays to ensure every family can celebrate with a meal. Contact us to learn about upcoming drives or to organize one in your community.

🤝 Community Partnerships

We're always looking for community partners — local businesses, churches, schools, and civic organizations — who want to make a difference. Whether it's hosting a food drive, sponsoring supplies, or volunteering as a group, there are many ways to get involved.

About Simply Give Double Match: For every $10 Simply Give card added to a customer order on Double Match Days, Meijer will add $20, resulting in a total $30 contribution. Purchases up to $5,000 on Double Match Day will be matched by Meijer up to a maximum donation of $10,000 per pantry/campaign.
