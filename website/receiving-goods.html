Who Can Receive Help?

We serve residents of Huron, Berlin Heights, and the 44839 zip code area who are experiencing food insecurity. This includes:

  • Low-income families and individuals
  • Single parents
  • Seniors on fixed incomes
  • Those who are unemployed or underemployed
  • People with disabilities
  • Anyone facing temporary hardship

Important: Through our partnership with Second Harvest Food Bank, you do not need to show proof of income to receive food. Your household just needs to fall within eligibility guidelines. If you're struggling to put food on the table, you likely qualify. When in doubt, come visit us — we're here to help, not to turn anyone away.

🌟 Your First Visit

  1. Arrive during operating hours — Walk-ins are welcome! No appointment needed for regular hours.
  2. Check in with a volunteer — We'll greet you and help you get started.
  3. Complete a brief registration — Just some basic information for our records.
  4. Receive your food — We'll help you select items for your household.
  5. That's it! — The whole process usually takes about 15-20 minutes.

Common Questions

How often can I receive food?

Visit frequency depends on our current policies. Please ask when you visit, or call us at 419-616-0088 for current guidelines.

Do I need to prove my income?

No. Through our partnership with Second Harvest Food Bank, we do not require proof of income. Your household should fall within eligibility guidelines, but we trust that if you need help, you qualify.

Can someone pick up food for me?

Yes, someone else can pick up food on your behalf. They'll need your information and proof of address for your household.

What if I don't have an ID?

Please come anyway and speak with us. We'll do our best to help you and figure out a solution together.

Do I need to be a US citizen?

No. We serve all members of our community regardless of citizenship status.

Is there a cost?

No. Our services are completely free to those in need.
