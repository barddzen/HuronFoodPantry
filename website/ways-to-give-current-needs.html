🗓️ Seasonal Needs

During different times of year, we have special needs:

Winter: Soup, hot cocoa, comfort foods

Summer: Kid-friendly snacks, easy meals (school's out!)

Holidays: Stuffing, cranberry sauce, dessert mixes, special treats

⚠️ Items We Cannot Accept

  • Expired food (please check dates before donating)
  • Opened or damaged packages
  • Homemade or home-canned items
  • Items without labels
  • Alcoholic beverages
  • Recalled products
  • Perishables without prior arrangement
