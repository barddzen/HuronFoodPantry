Our Mission

No neighbor should go hungry.

We provide food to help the hungry in our community — low-income families, single parents, those out of work, the elderly, the disabled, and anyone facing temporary hard times. Everyone deserves access to nutritious food, and we're here to help with dignity, respect, and open arms.

Who We Serve

Residents of Huron, Berlin Heights, and the 44839 zip code. Unsure if you qualify? Reach out — we're here to help, not to judge.
